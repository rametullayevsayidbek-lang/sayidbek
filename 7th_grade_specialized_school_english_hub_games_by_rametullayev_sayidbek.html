How to Cook Authentic Kungrad Juweri Gurtik (Besbarmak)

1. Introduction / Kirish

"Juweri Gurtik is the proud culinary symbol of Kungrad and Karakalpakstan. Rich, hearty, and full of tradition, this dish brings whole families together around a generous table!"

🇺🇿 Jo'xori Gurtik — Qo'ng'irot va Qoraqalpog'iston oshxonasining faxrli ramzidir. To'yimli va an'analarga boy bu taom butun oilani fayzli dasturxon atrofida jamlaydi!

2. Preparation Time / Vaqti
⏱️ 2 hours (120 daqiqa)
⏳
3. Ingredients / Masalliqlar
  • • 1 kg of bone-in beef or lamb 1 kg suyakli mol/qo'y go'shti
  • • 400g of white sorghum flour (jo'xori uni) 400g oq jo'xori uni
  • • 150g of wheat flour & 1 egg 150g bug'doy uni va 1 ta tuxum
  • • 3 large onions & 3 potatoes 3 ta yirik piyoz va 3 ta kartoshka
  • • 2 carrots & 1 cup of mung beans (mosh) 2 ta sabzi va 1 piyola mosh
  • • 1 tablespoon of salt & black pepper 1 osh qoshiq tuz va murch
4. You'll also need / Kerakli jihozlar
  • • 1 deep cauldron (qozon) and a skimmer (chuqur qozon va chovli)
  • • 1 rolling pin (o'qlov) and wooden board (o'qlov va taxtakach)
  • • 1 wide traditional platter (tabaq) (keng yog'och yoki chinni tabaq)
5. What to do / Tayyorlash tartibi (Imperative Verbs Highlighted)
  1. 1

    Place the meat in cold water in a deep cauldron, bring it to a boil, and skim off the foam carefully.

    Go'shtni chuqur qozondagi sovuq suvga soling, qaynating va ko'pigini ehtiyotkorlik bilan olib tashlang.

  2. 2

    Simmer the broth gently for 1.5 hours, then add the mung beans, whole potatoes, carrots, and salt.

    Sho'rvani 1,5 soat past olovda qaynating, so'ngra mosh, butun kartoshka, sabzi va tuzni soling.

  3. 3

    Mix the sorghum flour, wheat flour, egg, and warm salted water, and knead a firm, smooth dough.

    Jo'xori uni, bug'doy uni, tuxum va iliq tuzli suvni aralashtirib, qattiq va silliq xamir qoring.

  4. 4

    Roll out the dough to 3 mm thickness and cut it into neat diamond-shaped pieces (gurtik).

    Xamirni 3 mm qalinlikda yoying va chiroyli romb (gurtik) shakllarida kesib chiqing.

  5. 5

    Remove the cooked meat and vegetables, boil the gurtik in the rich broth for 7 minutes, and serve on a wide tabaq topped with sliced meat and onion sauce!

    Pishgan go'sht va sabzavotlarni suzib oling, gurtiklarni boy sho'rvada 7 daqiqa qaynating va keng tabaqqa solib, ustiga to'g'ralgan go'sht va piyozli sous quyib torting!

6. Chef Sayidbek's Note / Eslatma (Qo'ng'irot):

This recipe serves 5–6 people. If you want, you can serve the hot broth (sorpa) separately in bowls (keseler), but don't overcook the sorghum gurtik because it tastes best when it holds its tender shape!

🇺🇿 Bu retsept 5–6 kishi uchun mo'ljallangan. Agar xohlasangiz, issiq sho'rvasini alohida kosalarda tortishingiz mumkin, lekin jo'xori gurtikni haddan tashqari ko'p qaynatmang, chunki u o'z shaklini saqlab qolganda eng mazali bo'ladi!

        🔬 Unit 9 Language Lab Rules

        1. Imperative (Buyruq mayli): Use base verbs (Rinse, Slice, Heat, Add) to give clear instructions.
        2. Negative Imperative: Use Don't + verb (e.g., Don't stir the layers) to give warnings.
        3. Sequence (then): Use then to show the next action in order.
        4. Choice (If you want): Use If you want to give optional tips.
        5. Reason (because): Use because to explain why a step or warning is important.
